What this experiment can tell you

The exact solver tests whether the supplied noiseless parity data identifies the target. SGD tests a particular architecture and optimization budget. A failed SGD run shows a limitation of that configuration; it does not prove computational hardness.

Validation determines grid crossing intervals; an independent test draw reports errors. Crossings outside the sample grid stay censored. Confidence intervals in the download are pointwise, not simultaneous transition guarantees.

RESEARCH INTEGRITY

Assumptions are part of the result.

Post-hoc LI: the heuristic score includes test error; it cannot predict learnability before training. Random-label fitting correlation is an algorithm-dependent proxy, not a certified Rademacher supremum.

Oracle access: the Fourier diagnostic uses counterfactual queries to the hidden labeling function and known support size. It is separate from ordinary labeled-data diagnostics.

Leap: the corrected implementation measures new coordinates relative to the union of preceding monomials. It does not restrict them to a nested chain.

PAC-Bayes: the corrected calculation includes prior-grid selection and finite Monte Carlo uncertainty. It certifies a randomized Gibbs classifier. The old saved bound numbers and paper claims require revision.

Scope: synthetic noiseless tasks, three seeds, small networks. This is exploratory infrastructure, with no new theorem or verified claim of research novelty.

Foundations: Merged-staircase property, COLT 2022 · Leap complexity, COLT 2023
